Engineering Release Management Process

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Process

Engineering releases follow a two-week sprint cycle tracked in Jira. Release candidates for Project Atlas are reviewed by Fatima Sheikh, QA Lead, before deployment to AWS production environments by Siddharth Rao, DevOps Engineer.

All releases require sign-off from the Engineering Manager (Vishal Choudhary for Platform, Meenakshi Sundaram for Backend) and are documented in Confluence.
