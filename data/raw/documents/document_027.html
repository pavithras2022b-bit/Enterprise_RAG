Acceptable Use of IT Resources Policy

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Purpose

This document defines the Acceptable Use of IT Resources Policy (POL-IT-001) of Meridian Dynamics Inc.. The policy is owned and maintained by the Information Technology department under the direction of Suresh Pillai. It was last reviewed on 15 January 2026 and is scheduled for its next review on 15 January 2027.

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent, enforceable standards that apply to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc., ensuring alignment with Meridian Dynamics Inc.'s legal, regulatory and operational obligations.

Scope

This policy applies to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc.. All employees, contractors and interns of Meridian Dynamics Inc. operating out of Chennai, Bangalore, Pune, Austin, London, Singapore are expected to comply with this policy as a condition of continued engagement with the company.

Policy Details

Company-issued laptops, accounts and network access are provided for business purposes. Employees must not install unlicensed software; all software requests are routed through ServiceNow to the Information Technology department for review under the Software Procurement and Licensing Policy (POL-IT-003).

Roles and Responsibilities

Suresh Pillai is accountable for the overall administration of this policy within Information Technology.

Department Directors are responsible for ensuring compliance within their respective teams and for escalating exceptions to Information Technology.

All employees are responsible for reading, understanding and adhering to this policy as a condition of employment at Meridian Dynamics Inc..

Related Policies

This policy should be read in conjunction with the IT Asset Management Policy (POL-IT-002) and the Software Procurement and Licensing Policy (POL-IT-003).
