Sales Deal Desk Approval Process

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Process

Non-standard pricing or contract terms proposed by Account Executives (Laura Bennett, Michael Chen) are reviewed by Anand Krishnan, Sales Manager, Enterprise Accounts, and require Legal sign-off from Vidya Raman for deals above INR 1,00,00,000.

Approved deals are recorded in Salesforce and reported weekly to Arjun Menon, Director of Sales.
