Project Vega: Project Charter

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Project Overview

Project Vega is an enterprise accounts expansion initiative targeting the North American market. The project is owned by the Sales department and sponsored by Arjun Menon.

Anand Krishnan serves as the project lead, coordinating a team consisting of Laura Bennett, Michael Chen.

Systems and Tools

Project Vega relies on Salesforce as its core systems. Salesforce is maintained by the Sales department.

Status and Governance

Progress on Project Vega is reviewed monthly by Arjun Menon and reported to the Sales department's leadership team. Documentation is maintained in Confluence and tasks are tracked in Jira where applicable.
