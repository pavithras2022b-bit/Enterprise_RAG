Annual Security Audit Summary

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Summary

The FY2025-26 security audit, led by Meera Krishnan with support from Tarun Sethi, reviewed access controls across Okta SSO, AWS and Splunk SIEM. No critical findings were identified; two medium findings related to stale AWS access grants were remediated by the Engineering and Information Technology departments within 30 days.
