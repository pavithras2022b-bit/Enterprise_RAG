Employee Benefits Overview

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Available Benefits

Group Health Insurance: covers employee, spouse and up to two dependent children with a sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per family.

Life and Accident Insurance: provides coverage equal to three times annual base salary.

Provident Fund: employer and employee contribute 12% of basic salary each month.

Meal Vouchers: monthly meal card allowance of INR 2,200 credited via the payroll system.

Wellness Reimbursement: annual reimbursement of up to INR 15,000 for gym memberships, fitness classes or wellness apps.

Parental Leave Top-up: salary top-up during statutory maternity and paternity leave periods.

Learning Stipend: annual stipend of INR 30,000 for certifications, courses and conference attendance.

Employee Stock Purchase Plan: eligible employees may purchase company stock at a 15% discount twice a year.

Relocation Assistance: one-time relocation allowance for employees transferring between Meridian Dynamics offices.

Employee Assistance Program: confidential counseling and mental health support available 24x7 through a third-party partner.

Administration

All benefits are administered by the Human Resources department through Workday HRMS. Questions regarding eligibility should be directed to Sanjay Verma, Senior Manager, Compensation & Benefits, who reports to Divya Ramaswamy.
