Code of Conduct Policy

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Purpose

This document defines the Code of Conduct Policy (POL-HR-005) of Meridian Dynamics Inc.. The policy is owned and maintained by the Human Resources department under the direction of Divya Ramaswamy. It was last reviewed on 15 January 2026 and is scheduled for its next review on 15 January 2027.

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent, enforceable standards that apply to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc., ensuring alignment with Meridian Dynamics Inc.'s legal, regulatory and operational obligations.

Scope

This policy applies to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc.. All employees, contractors and interns of Meridian Dynamics Inc. operating out of Chennai, Bangalore, Pune, Austin, London, Singapore are expected to comply with this policy as a condition of continued engagement with the company.

Policy Details

All employees are expected to act with integrity, avoid conflicts of interest, and report violations to their manager, HR Business Partner Kavya Reddy, or through the confidential ethics hotline managed by the Legal department under Nithya Subramaniam.

Roles and Responsibilities

Divya Ramaswamy is accountable for the overall administration of this policy within Human Resources.

Department Directors are responsible for ensuring compliance within their respective teams and for escalating exceptions to Human Resources.

All employees are responsible for reading, understanding and adhering to this policy as a condition of employment at Meridian Dynamics Inc..

Related Policies

This policy should be read in conjunction with the Leave and Attendance Policy (POL-HR-001) and the Employee Benefits Policy (POL-HR-002).
