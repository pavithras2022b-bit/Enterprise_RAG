Project Titan: Project Charter

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Project Overview

Project Titan is a process automation initiative streamlining cross-department operational workflows. The project is owned by the Operations department and sponsored by Farhan Ahmed.

Karan Malhotra serves as the project lead, coordinating a team consisting of Ayesha Siddiqui.

Systems and Tools

Project Titan relies on ServiceNow, Coupa as its core systems. ServiceNow is maintained by the Information Technology department. Coupa is maintained by the Procurement department.

Status and Governance

Progress on Project Titan is reviewed monthly by Farhan Ahmed and reported to the Operations department's leadership team. Documentation is maintained in Confluence and tasks are tracked in Jira where applicable.
