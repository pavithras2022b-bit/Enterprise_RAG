Work From Home Program Overview

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Overview

Meridian Dynamics Inc.'s Work From Home program, governed by POL-HR-008, allows eligible employees in Engineering, Information Technology, Customer Support, Marketing and Finance to work remotely up to 3 days per week.

The program is jointly overseen by Human Resources under Divya Ramaswamy and Information Security under Meera Krishnan, who ensures remote access complies with the Information Security Policy (POL-SEC-001).
