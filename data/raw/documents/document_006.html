Engineering Department Overview

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Overview

The Engineering department at Meridian Dynamics Inc. is headquartered in Bangalore, Karnataka, India (Engineering Campus) and is identified internally by the department code ENG. The department is led by Karthik Iyer, who holds the title of Director of Engineering and reports to Vikram Suresh.

Engineering operates as one of the 13 core departments of Meridian Dynamics Inc., alongside Human Resources, Information Technology, Information Security, Finance and other functions, and coordinates closely with these departments on cross-functional initiatives.

Projects Owned

Engineering manages Project Atlas, a platform migration initiative moving the core order-management system to a microservices architecture on AWS. The project is sponsored by Karthik Iyer and led by Vishal Choudhary, with contributions from Rachel Fernandes, Nikita Joshi, Siddharth Rao, Fatima Sheikh. Project Atlas relies on Jira, Confluence, AWS as its primary systems.

Systems Maintained

Jira is maintained by the Engineering department and is used for software project and issue tracking.

Confluence is maintained by the Engineering department and is used for internal documentation and knowledge sharing.

AWS is maintained by the Engineering department and is used for cloud infrastructure hosting production and staging environments.

Contact and Escalation

For matters concerning Engineering, employees should first contact their reporting manager or Karthik Iyer directly. Escalations that cannot be resolved within the department are routed to the office of the Chief Executive Officer, Chief Executive Officer Ananya Raghunathan, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (Global Headquarters).
