Employee Benefits Policy

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Purpose

This document defines the Employee Benefits Policy (POL-HR-002) of Meridian Dynamics Inc.. The policy is owned and maintained by the Human Resources department under the direction of Divya Ramaswamy. It was last reviewed on 15 January 2026 and is scheduled for its next review on 15 January 2027.

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent, enforceable standards that apply to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc., ensuring alignment with Meridian Dynamics Inc.'s legal, regulatory and operational obligations.

Scope

This policy applies to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc.. All employees, contractors and interns of Meridian Dynamics Inc. operating out of Chennai, Bangalore, Pune, Austin, London, Singapore are expected to comply with this policy as a condition of continued engagement with the company.

Policy Details

Group Health Insurance: covers employee, spouse and up to two dependent children with a sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per family.

Life and Accident Insurance: provides coverage equal to three times annual base salary.

Provident Fund: employer and employee contribute 12% of basic salary each month.

Meal Vouchers: monthly meal card allowance of INR 2,200 credited via the payroll system.

Wellness Reimbursement: annual reimbursement of up to INR 15,000 for gym memberships, fitness classes or wellness apps.

Parental Leave Top-up: salary top-up during statutory maternity and paternity leave periods.

Learning Stipend: annual stipend of INR 30,000 for certifications, courses and conference attendance.

Employee Stock Purchase Plan: eligible employees may purchase company stock at a 15% discount twice a year.

Relocation Assistance: one-time relocation allowance for employees transferring between Meridian Dynamics offices.

Employee Assistance Program: confidential counseling and mental health support available 24x7 through a third-party partner.

Benefits enrollment and management is administered by the Human Resources department through Workday HRMS, and eligibility questions should be directed to Sanjay Verma, Senior Manager, Compensation & Benefits.

Roles and Responsibilities

Divya Ramaswamy is accountable for the overall administration of this policy within Human Resources.

Department Directors are responsible for ensuring compliance within their respective teams and for escalating exceptions to Human Resources.

All employees are responsible for reading, understanding and adhering to this policy as a condition of employment at Meridian Dynamics Inc..

Related Policies

This policy should be read in conjunction with the Leave and Attendance Policy (POL-HR-001) and the Recruitment and Onboarding Policy (POL-HR-003).
