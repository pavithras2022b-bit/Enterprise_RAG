Information Security Department Overview

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Overview

The Information Security department at Meridian Dynamics Inc. is headquartered in Bangalore, Karnataka, India (Engineering Campus) and is identified internally by the department code INFOSEC. The department is led by Meera Krishnan, who holds the title of Chief Information Security Officer and reports to Vikram Suresh.

Information Security operates as one of the 13 core departments of Meridian Dynamics Inc., alongside Human Resources, Information Technology, Finance, Procurement and other functions, and coordinates closely with these departments on cross-functional initiatives.

Team Structure

The Information Security department currently comprises 4 employees on record, including:

- Anjali Krishnamurthy (Security Operations Manager, Information Security department, based in Bangalore) reports to Meera Krishnan.

- Tarun Sethi (Security Compliance Lead, Information Security department, based in Bangalore) reports to Meera Krishnan.

- Ishita Banerjee (Incident Response Lead, Information Security department, based in Bangalore) reports to Meera Krishnan.

Projects Owned

Information Security manages Project Nimbus, a zero-trust security architecture rollout across all regional offices. The project is sponsored by Meera Krishnan and led by Anjali Krishnamurthy, with contributions from Tarun Sethi, Ishita Banerjee. Project Nimbus relies on Splunk SIEM, Okta SSO, Bitwarden Enterprise as its primary systems.

Systems Maintained

Okta SSO is maintained by the Information Security department and is used for single sign-on and identity and access management.

Meridian VPN Gateway is maintained by the Information Security department and is used for secure remote access to internal networks.

Splunk SIEM is maintained by the Information Security department and is used for security information and event monitoring.

Bitwarden Enterprise is maintained by the Information Security department and is used for enterprise password management.

Policies Owned

Information Security Policy (POL-SEC-001) is owned by Information Security and applies to all departments.

Password and Access Management Policy (POL-SEC-002) is owned by Information Security and applies to all departments.

Data Classification and Handling Policy (POL-SEC-003) is owned by Information Security and applies to all departments.

Incident Response Policy (POL-SEC-004) is owned by Information Security and applies to Information Technology, Engineering, Information Security.

Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy (POL-SEC-005) is owned by Information Security and applies to all departments.

Contact and Escalation

For matters concerning Information Security, employees should first contact their reporting manager or Meera Krishnan directly. Escalations that cannot be resolved within the department are routed to the office of the Chief Executive Officer, Chief Executive Officer Ananya Raghunathan, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (Global Headquarters).
