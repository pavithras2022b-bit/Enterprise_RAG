Project Helios: Project Charter

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Project Overview

Project Helios is an applied AI research initiative building a recommendation engine for enterprise customers. The project is owned by the Research & Development department and sponsored by Aditi Deshmukh.

Nandini Krishnan serves as the project lead, coordinating a team consisting of Thomas Mueller, Abhishek Ghosh.

Systems and Tools

Project Helios relies on Jira, Confluence as its core systems. Jira is maintained by the Engineering department. Confluence is maintained by the Engineering department.

Status and Governance

Progress on Project Helios is reviewed monthly by Aditi Deshmukh and reported to the Research & Development department's leadership team. Documentation is maintained in Confluence and tasks are tracked in Jira where applicable.
