New Employee Device Provisioning Procedure

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Procedure

Upon receipt of a confirmed offer in Workday HRMS, the Information Technology department under Suresh Pillai provisions a laptop, Okta SSO account and Microsoft 365 license ahead of the employee's start date.

Devices are tagged in ServiceNow per the IT Asset Management Policy (POL-IT-002) and assigned to the employee's cost center for the owning department.
