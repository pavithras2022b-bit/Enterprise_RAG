Overview

The Legal department at Meridian Dynamics Inc. is headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (Global Headquarters) and is identified internally by the department code LEGAL. The department is led by Nithya Subramaniam, who holds the title of Director of Legal and reports to Ananya Raghunathan.
