Data Classification and Handling Policy

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Purpose

This document defines the Data Classification and Handling Policy (POL-SEC-003) of Meridian Dynamics Inc.. The policy is owned and maintained by the Information Security department under the direction of Meera Krishnan. It was last reviewed on 15 January 2026 and is scheduled for its next review on 15 January 2027.

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent, enforceable standards that apply to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc., ensuring alignment with Meridian Dynamics Inc.'s legal, regulatory and operational obligations.

Scope

This policy applies to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc.. All employees, contractors and interns of Meridian Dynamics Inc. operating out of Chennai, Bangalore, Pune, Austin, London, Singapore are expected to comply with this policy as a condition of continued engagement with the company.

Policy Details

Data at Meridian Dynamics is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data includes employee PII, payroll records and customer financial data.

Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit, and access is logged through Splunk SIEM, monitored by the Information Security department.

Roles and Responsibilities

Meera Krishnan is accountable for the overall administration of this policy within Information Security.

Department Directors are responsible for ensuring compliance within their respective teams and for escalating exceptions to Information Security.

All employees are responsible for reading, understanding and adhering to this policy as a condition of employment at Meridian Dynamics Inc..

Related Policies

This policy should be read in conjunction with the Information Security Policy (POL-SEC-001) and the Password and Access Management Policy (POL-SEC-002).
