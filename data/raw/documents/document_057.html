Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Overview

The Network Operations team, led by Ravi Shankar and reporting to Suresh Pillai, manages connectivity across all Meridian Dynamics Inc. offices including Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (Global Headquarters), Bangalore, Karnataka, India (Engineering Campus), Pune, Maharashtra, India (Delivery Center), Austin, Texas, USA (Americas Regional Office), London, United Kingdom (EMEA Regional Office) and Singapore (APAC Regional Office).

The team maintains the Meridian VPN Gateway in coordination with the Information Security department and supports Project Orion's data center consolidation effort.
