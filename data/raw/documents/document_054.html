IT Onboarding Checklist

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Checklist

1. Provision laptop and peripherals (Information Technology).

2. Create Okta SSO identity and assign Microsoft 365 license (Information Technology).

3. Grant access to department-specific systems (Jira and Confluence for Engineering; Salesforce for Sales; Coupa for Procurement) upon manager request via ServiceNow.

4. Complete mandatory security awareness training within 30 days, tracked by the Information Security department.
