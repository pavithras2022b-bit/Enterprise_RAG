Customer Support Department Overview

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Overview

The Customer Support department at Meridian Dynamics Inc. is headquartered in Singapore (APAC Regional Office) and is identified internally by the department code CS. The department is led by Wei Ling Tan, who holds the title of Director of Customer Support and reports to Ananya Raghunathan.

Customer Support operates as one of the 13 core departments of Meridian Dynamics Inc., alongside Human Resources, Information Technology, Information Security, Finance and other functions, and coordinates closely with these departments on cross-functional initiatives.

Team Structure

The Customer Support department currently comprises 3 employees on record, including:

- Li Wei (Customer Support Manager, Customer Support department, based in Singapore) reports to Wei Ling Tan.

- Priyanka Das (Customer Support Specialist, Customer Support department, based in Singapore) reports to Li Wei.

Contact and Escalation

For matters concerning Customer Support, employees should first contact their reporting manager or Wei Ling Tan directly. Escalations that cannot be resolved within the department are routed to the office of the Chief Executive Officer, Chief Executive Officer Ananya Raghunathan, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (Global Headquarters).
