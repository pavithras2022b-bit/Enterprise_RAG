Travel and Expense Policy

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Purpose

This document defines the Travel and Expense Policy (POL-FIN-001) of Meridian Dynamics Inc.. The policy is owned and maintained by the Finance department under the direction of Lakshmi Narayanan. It was last reviewed on 15 January 2026 and is scheduled for its next review on 15 January 2027.

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent, enforceable standards that apply to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc., ensuring alignment with Meridian Dynamics Inc.'s legal, regulatory and operational obligations.

Scope

This policy applies to all departments across Meridian Dynamics Inc.. All employees, contractors and interns of Meridian Dynamics Inc. operating out of Chennai, Bangalore, Pune, Austin, London, Singapore are expected to comply with this policy as a condition of continued engagement with the company.

Policy Details

All business travel must be booked through SAP Concur. Domestic economy class is standard for trips under 6 hours; international travel over 6 hours permits premium economy with Director-level approval.

Per diem rates are INR 3,500 for domestic travel and USD 90 for international travel. Expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of travel completion and are approved by the employee's reporting manager, then processed by Harish Venkataraman's Accounts Payable team.

Roles and Responsibilities

Lakshmi Narayanan is accountable for the overall administration of this policy within Finance.

Department Directors are responsible for ensuring compliance within their respective teams and for escalating exceptions to Finance.

All employees are responsible for reading, understanding and adhering to this policy as a condition of employment at Meridian Dynamics Inc..

Related Policies

This policy should be read in conjunction with the Purchase Requisition and Approval Policy (POL-FIN-003) and the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-FIN-004).
