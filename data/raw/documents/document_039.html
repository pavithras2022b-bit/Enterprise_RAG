Manager's Guide to Leave Approvals

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Guide

Managers are expected to respond to leave requests in Workday HRMS within 2 business days.

For Casual Leave and Sick Leave, direct manager approval is sufficient. For Sabbatical Leave, approval from both the reporting manager and the department Director is required, and Human Resources under Divya Ramaswamy must be notified at least 60 days in advance.
