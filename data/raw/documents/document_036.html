Attendance Tracking Procedure

Meridian Dynamics Inc. — Internal Document

Procedure

Attendance for all employees, including those in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (Global Headquarters) and Bangalore, Karnataka, India (Engineering Campus), is tracked through Workday HRMS using biometric check-in at office locations and self-declared status for remote work days under the Work From Home Policy (POL-HR-008).

Managers review attendance discrepancies weekly. Repeated unexplained absences are escalated to the HR Business Partner assigned to the department for further action.
